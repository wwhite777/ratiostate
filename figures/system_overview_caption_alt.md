# Figure 1 caption and alt text

**Caption.** Figure 1. Recurrent storage-precision design. (A) At each teacher-forced MNIST position, retained matrix and normalizer states are expanded to FP32 before the unchanged eight-layer, eight-head graph updates the recurrence within each attention head (Ŝ and ẑ denote the decoded feeds); the graph uses raw current S and z for its inclusive readout and 30-parameter next-pixel prediction, then the returned states are encoded for the next call. (B) Four crossed FP32/BF16 choices for matrix S and normalizer z are compared with ordinary FP16 and two-word BF16 controls. (C) Ten development images precede the fixed six-arm evaluation on 32 disjoint images; 783 saved predictions per image are evaluated with a separately defined conditional discretized logistic-mixture score. The diagram describes numerical state storage between calls, not a packed deployment kernel.

**Alt text.** Three-panel schematic. Panel A shows teacher-forced MNIST pixel and position entering an unchanged FP32 model together with retained states that were expanded to FP32 before the call. Within each attention head, the matrix and normalizer recurrence begins from decoded stored feeds marked with hats, then produces an inclusive readout from raw updated states and 30 parameters for ten next-pixel mixture components. A separate arrow carries returned states through a format encoder into a retained cache, then loops back through FP32 expansion for the next model call. Panel B lists the six formats: FP32, S-BF16, z-BF16, joint BF16, FP16, and two-word BF16. Panel C shows ten development images, locking of the six-arm comparison, 32 disjoint evaluation images, 783 predictions per image, and a separate FP64 conditional score.
